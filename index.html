AADI KAILASH & OM PARVAT

“Tucked away in the majestic Himalayas, Adi Kailash is a sacred gem waiting to be discovered.

This lesser-known counterpart of the revered Mount Kailash in Tibet shares a similar legend and spiritual significance.

Adi Kailash, also known as Chota Kailash, is a breathtaking peak nestled in the Pithoragarh district of Uttarakhand, India.

This natural wonder is shrouded in mystique, with its snow-capped peak glistening like a crown jewel against the azure sky.

As you embark on the Adi Kailash Yatra, get ready to be enchanted by the surreal beauty of the Himalayas, and experience a journey that will awaken your soul and leave you spellbound.”
